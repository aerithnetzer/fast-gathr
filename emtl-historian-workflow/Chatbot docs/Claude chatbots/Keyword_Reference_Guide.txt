KEYWORD REFERENCE GUIDE (v2)

PURPOSE AND STATUS OF THE REGISTRY
Keywords prioritize commercial practices and fraudulent or deceptive activity. The Keyword
Registry is now MATURE: it provides broad, settled coverage of the research interests below across
five areas — Commerce Practice; Goods & Commodity; Credit & Debt; Honesty/Ethics/Reputation; and
Social/Cultural dimensions of trade. Because the registry is mature, the default posture has
changed: PREFER AN EXISTING KEYWORD. Reach for the Registry first and tag from it; treat the
creation of a new keyword as the rare exception governed by the narrow rules at the end of this
guide, NOT as a routine response to an unlisted detail.

AREAS OF INTEREST (what keywords are FOR)
Assign keywords (from the Registry) that illuminate:
- Trading and contracting practices — local, regional, or international.
- Markets, fairs, and trade conducted in shops, streets, shambles, ports, docks, bourses, and any
  other place of exchange.
- Trade in specific commodities, and the practices distinctive to particular trades.
- Trading companies and their trade abroad.
- Relationships among merchants, factors, and brokers, and the hiring of and contracting with them.
- The activities of commercial actors: merchants, artisans, traders, brokers, factors, apprentices,
  female traders, alien merchants, partnerships, bankrupts.
- Commercial dispute resolution through mediation and arbitration (as lived experience, not
  procedure).
- The failure of credit: imprisonment for debt, bankruptcy, compounding of debt, absconding debtors,
  pledge and pawn.
- Fraudulent or deceitful practices, schemes, card-sharps, swindles, forgeries, and fraudulent
  conveyances for sham or no consideration done to hinder, delay, or defraud creditors.
- A person trusting, perhaps unwisely, and that trust being violated.
- A person's reputation or creditworthiness.

These orient ATTENTION only. They do not justify inference, conclusion, or overstatement, and they
do not by themselves justify a new keyword — each of these areas is already represented in the
Registry.

USING THE REGISTRY (the default path)
- Consult the Keyword Registry and select the existing keyword(s) the document's substance supports.
- A keyword applies only when its concept is expressly named or clearly described in the document,
  with verbatim evidence; never on inference, implication, or probability alone.
- Two related keywords at different levels of abstraction may both apply (e.g. a specific trade AND
  the general practice); do not drop the higher-level category because a specific one is present.
- Do NOT broaden, reinterpret, or stretch a keyword's meaning to fit, and do NOT invent near-
  synonyms for a listed keyword. If the fit requires bending the definition, the keyword does not
  apply.
- Under-generation is correct: a document may warrant zero, one, or two keywords. Do not pad.

CREATING A NEW KEYWORD (the narrow exception)
The registry is broad and settled, so new keywords are now the EXCEPTION, not a routine output.
Before proposing one, confirm that NO existing keyword — at either a specific OR a general level of
abstraction — covers the concept, and that the gap is real rather than a wording mismatch with an
existing keyword.

There are exactly TWO openings under which a new keyword may be proposed. These are exhaustive: a
candidate that does not fall squarely within Opening 1 or Opening 2 is not a new keyword — tag
existing keywords, or none.

  OPENING 1 — A NEW TRADE OR COMMODITY CLASS (the primary, expected opening).
  The registry deliberately differentiates trade practices, so a trade or commodity class not yet
  represented may earn its own Goods & Commodity keyword once the document gives concentrated
  material on that trade (not a passing mention). The existing trade keywords are the model and the
  level of abstraction: Cloth Trade, Wool Trade, Spice Trade, Sugar Trade, Metals Trade. A new one
  must sit at that SAME level — a trade or commodity CLASS (e.g. a Leather Trade, a Wine Trade, a
  Victualling Trade, a Timber Trade), NOT a single named commodity or product, and NOT a sub-type of
  a trade already keyed.

  OPENING 2 — A GENUINELY NEW COMMERCIAL MECHANISM (rare; high bar).
  A distinct commercial process or device not reducible to any existing keyword may be proposed, but
  only when ALL of the following hold:
    (a) it is a recurring KIND of commercial practice, mechanism, or relationship — not a one-off
        fact, not a wrong/offence (discrete wrongs are handled by event-tagging, not keywords),
        and not a procedural or litigation artifact;
    (b) it is clearly and specifically distinguished from EVERY existing keyword, including the
        broad ones (Fraud, Reckoning, Contract Term, Network, Custom, Market Regulation,
        Partnership, Factorage, Trusting, Reputation), so that it is not a synonym, sub-case, or
        re-description of one already listed; and
    (c) it sits at the SAME level of abstraction as existing keywords — neither narrower than a
        single instance nor broader than the existing categories.
  If a candidate mechanism can be expressed by tagging one or two existing keywords (even imperfectly),
  do NOT create a new keyword; tag the existing ones.

OPENINGS THAT ARE NOW EFFECTIVELY CLOSED
- New SOCIAL/CULTURAL keywords: the social/cultural dimension is already represented (Female
  Merchant, Literacy, Insult, Gossip, Sleep, Time of Day, Quoted Speech, Translators). Do not add to
  this set absent an exceptional, clearly distinct, recurring phenomenon; default to existing.
- Single commodities or products, single named schemes, regional variants, or one-off details:
  these are NOT keywords. Tag the relevant existing keyword(s) instead, or none.
- Anything expressible as a combination of existing keywords: tag the combination; do not coin a new
  term for the intersection.

NEW-KEYWORD PROPOSAL DISCIPLINE
- Propose sparingly. In most documents the correct number of new keywords is zero.
- A proposal must name which opening it falls under (Opening 1 or Opening 2), state why no existing
  keyword (specific or general) covers it, and give the verbatim triggering evidence.
- A proposal is a suggestion for human review, not a self-approved addition to the Registry.
